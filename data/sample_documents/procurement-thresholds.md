# Procurement Thresholds

> Fabricated sample content for the Digital Commons beta. The City of Example does
> not exist, and these are not real procurement rules.

## Purpose

These thresholds set how much competition and approval a City of Example purchase
needs, based on its total expected cost including tax and delivery.

## Thresholds

Purchases under $5,000 need one written quote. Purchases from $5,000 to $49,999 need
three written quotes. Purchases from $50,000 to $249,999 need a formal request for
quotations posted for ten working days. Purchases of $250,000 or more need a sealed
competitive bid, posted for at least twenty working days.

## Approval authority

Department heads may approve purchases up to $49,999. The Finance Director approves
purchases up to $249,999. City Council approves anything at or above $250,000.

## Sole source purchases

A sole source purchase is allowed only when one vendor can meet the need. The
department must file a written justification, and the Finance Director must approve
it before any order is placed.

## Splitting purchases

Dividing a purchase to stay under a threshold is prohibited. Related orders placed
within ninety days count as one purchase when the threshold is applied.
