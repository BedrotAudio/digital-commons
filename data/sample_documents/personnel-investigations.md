# Personnel Investigations Procedure

> Fabricated sample content for the Digital Commons beta. The City of Example does
> not exist, and this is not a real procedure.

## Purpose

This procedure guides Human Resources investigators through complaints of employee
misconduct at the City of Example.

## Intake

A complaint is logged within one working day. The HR Director assigns an
investigator who has no reporting relationship with anyone involved.

## Interviews

The investigator interviews the complainant first, then witnesses, then the
respondent. Each person may bring a representative. Interview notes are stored in
the restricted HR case file and nowhere else.

## Administrative leave

The HR Director may place a respondent on paid administrative leave while an
investigation continues, if their presence could affect witnesses or evidence.

## Findings

The investigator delivers written findings within thirty working days. Findings are
shared only with the HR Director, the City Attorney and the deciding manager.
