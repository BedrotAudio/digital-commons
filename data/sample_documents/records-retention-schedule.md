# Records Retention Schedule

> Fabricated sample content for the Digital Commons beta. The City of Example does
> not exist, and this is not a real retention schedule.

## Purpose

This schedule states how long City of Example departments keep common records, and
when those records may be destroyed.

## General correspondence

Routine email and correspondence are kept for two years. Correspondence that sets
policy or commits City funds is kept for seven years.

## Financial records

Invoices, purchase orders and payment records are kept for seven years after the
end of the fiscal year. Audit reports are kept permanently.

## Personnel records

Personnel files are kept for six years after an employee leaves. Payroll records are
kept for seven years. Records of workplace injury are kept for thirty years.

## Legal holds

A legal hold overrides this schedule. Once the City Attorney issues a hold, affected
records must not be destroyed until the hold is released in writing.

## Destruction

The City Clerk approves destruction of records. Confidential paper records are
shredded, and a certificate of destruction is kept permanently.
