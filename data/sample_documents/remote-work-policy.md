# Remote Work Policy

> Fabricated sample content for the Digital Commons beta. The City of Example does
> not exist, and this is not a real policy.

## Purpose

This policy explains how City of Example employees may work away from their
assigned worksite while keeping services available to residents.

## Eligibility

Employees may request remote work after completing six months of service. The
position must not require daily in-person contact with the public, and the
employee must have a satisfactory performance review on file. Supervisors decide
eligibility position by position, not person by person.

## Schedule and core hours

Approved staff may work remotely up to three days per week. Core hours are 9:30 a.m.
to 3:30 p.m. Employees must be reachable by phone and chat during core hours, and
must attend in person whenever a supervisor gives two working days of notice.

## Equipment and security

The City provides a laptop, and employees provide a reliable home connection.
Confidential records must not be printed at home. City work must be done only on
City devices, and public Wi-Fi must be used only through the City VPN.

## Ending an arrangement

Either the employee or the supervisor may end a remote work arrangement with ten
working days of written notice. Arrangements are reviewed every twelve months.
